How booking works

No accounts, no apps — just four simple steps.

1

Pick a room & term

Choose the room you like and a lease term. Add a parking permit if you need one. The total is calculated instantly.

2

Submit your booking

Enter your name, contact info, and move-in date. You get a booking reference code right away.

3

Pay with Zelle

Send the total via Zelle to the number on your confirmation. Put your name and reference code in the memo.

4

Track & get confirmed

Use the Track booking page to check your status anytime. Once payment arrives, you're confirmed.

Good to know

Questions, answered

The things tenants ask us most. Anything else — just call or text.

How do I pay for my booking?

With Zelle. After you submit a booking you'll see the exact total, our Zelle number, and your booking reference code. Send the total via Zelle and put your name plus the reference code in the memo so we can match it. We confirm within about 24 hours of receiving it.

What's included in the rent?

It varies by room — each listing shows its amenities. Most rooms include utilities and high-speed WiFi. Any security deposit is shown on the room page before you book.

What lease terms can I choose?

Each room page lists the lease terms available and the monthly rate for each — longer stays often come with a lower monthly rate. The full total for your term is calculated for you before you commit.

Can I add parking?

Yes — tick the parking permit checkbox while booking. The monthly permit fee is added to your total automatically.

Can I see the room in person first?

Of course. Call or text us and we'll arrange a viewing at a time that suits you.

Are pets allowed?

No, sorry — pets aren't allowed in our rooms.

Still have questions? Call or text us.
